Connect your account

Have your Alive5 and Zapier accounts ready, with permission to connect them. For outbound texts, you’ll also need an SMS business number and a user assigned to its channel.

  1. Add Alive5 to a Zap

    Create a Zap, choose Alive5 as the trigger or action app, and select an event from the lists below.

  2. Connect your Alive5 account

    In the account step, add a connection and follow Zapier’s sign-in prompts. You can manage it later in App Connections ↗. Follow the account connection guide ↗ if the connection window doesn’t open.

  3. Map fields and test

    Select the requested channel, load a sample, and map its values into the next step. Review the test output before publishing your Zap. Testing a sending action can send a real text; use a phone number you control.

Working with incoming texts?

SMS Conversation covers both sent and received messages. Inspect your sample and filter for the direction you need before adding a reply action, so your own outgoing texts don’t trigger a loop.

Build your first Zap

Start with a calendar reminder. Open the Google Calendar template ↗, then connect both accounts.

  1. Choose the calendar and when the reminder should run.
  2. In the Alive5 action, select your business line, channel, and assigned user.
  3. Map a recipient’s phone number into To; a calendar attendee’s email address cannot receive an SMS. Compose your message using event details.
  4. Test with your own number, confirm the message arrives, and publish when the mappings are correct.

Only automate texts to recipients who have agreed to hear from your business. Use Zap History to inspect failures before rerunning a send.
