  3. Choose your business number

    Select an SMS line, then test the workflow with a phone number you control.

Triggers and actions

SMS received

Incoming message trigger

Starts a workflow when a text arrives at the selected business number. Needs one field, Business number.

Image-only messages do not start this trigger.

SMS received periodically

polling · 5 minute interval

Checks the same line on a schedule and includes image-only messages. Use it when delivery matters more than latency; new texts can take up to five minutes to appear.

What a trigger passes on

Field Example

Actions

Send SMS

Sends a text from a business line. Fields: From number, To number, Message, and Send as, the teammate credited in the shared inbox. Sends a real text; attachments are not supported.

Get received messages

Lists received messages from recent conversations, optionally filtered to one business number. For reporting and catch-up steps rather than live routing.

Get channels

Lists your channels and their assigned teammates. Mostly used behind the scenes to populate the number and teammate dropdowns.

Configure an example reply

  1. Add SMS received and select your business number.
  2. Add Send SMS. Map from_phone to the recipient, then choose your sending number and user.
  3. Write a reply template and test it with a phone you control.

The animated examples illustrate a fixed reply configured by you.

Working with messages

  • The received-message triggers provide received_at in ISO format and received_at_ms in epoch milliseconds.
  • The periodic trigger checks every five minutes and includes image-only texts.
  • After a send times out, check conversation history before retrying.
