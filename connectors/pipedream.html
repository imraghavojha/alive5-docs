Trigger workflows from SMS. Add code wherever you need it.

Connect your account

  1. Add Alive5

    In Pipedream, add the Alive5 connector to your workflow.

  2. Connect with an API key

    Copy your key from Alive5 Integrations and paste it into the connection.

  3. Choose your business number

    Select an SMS line. For outbound texts, choose a user assigned to that channel.

If Alive5 is not in your app picker, contact Alive5 support for connector access.

Triggers and actions

TRIGGER

New SMS Received

Starts when a text arrives at the selected number. Choose the SMS number when setting up the trigger.

ACTION

Send SMS

Choose a business number and sending user. Enter the recipient with a country code and your message.

ACTION

List Channels and Users

Get SMS channels and their assigned users for later workflow steps.

Build a reply workflow

  1. Add New SMS Received and choose your line.
  2. Add Send SMS. Map the incoming sender to the recipient.
  3. Enter a reply, then test with a phone number you control.
When enabled in Pipedream, this workflow sends real SMS. A successful result confirms API acceptance, not carrier delivery.

Usage notes

  • Image-only messages do not trigger a workflow.
  • After a send times out, check conversation history before retrying.
